## Findings

- Every case the analytic step covered ran faster than `r,s`, cold: 0.09-0.82 of its time.
- Fitted over those cases, the analytic time grows as ODE states^1.32 x (1 + directions)^1.51
  and the `r,s` time as ODE states^1.14 x p^1.98 x (1 + etas)^1.17 (p the covariance
  parameters), so their ratio grows only as ODE states^0.18 and falls as p grows.
- The slow analytic runs on long transit chains with pure proportional error were declines:
  the near-zero-prediction check ran only after the augmented model was compiled and solved
  (85-1170 s), then `r,s` ran anyway.
